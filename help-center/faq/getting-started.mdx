## Starting Your Project

1. Log in at `https://app.codecraftters.com`.
2. Click "New Project" on your dashboard.
3. Enter a name and description for the project.
4. Choose a documentation template or start blank.
5. Click "Create" to set up your space.

## Next Steps

Start adding pages and organize your docs in the editor. Your project auto-saves as you write.
