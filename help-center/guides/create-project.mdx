---

## Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dashboard">
    Log in to `https://app.codecraftters.com` and access your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Project">
    Click "New Project", name your project, and select a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Pages">
    Use the editor to add pages and sections. Click "+ Page" for new content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click "Publish" when ready. Your documentation is live!
  </Step>
</Steps>
