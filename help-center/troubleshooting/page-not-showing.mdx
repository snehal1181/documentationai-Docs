---
title: Page Not Showing After Publish
description: Fix missing documentation pages on CodeCraftters.
---

## Issue

- Published page does not appear in the sidebar/menu
- URL shows 404 Not Found

## Possible Causes

- Page is not set to "Public"
- Navigation cache not updated

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check page status">
    Go to your project. Confirm the page is marked "Public" in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh navigation">
    Click the "Sync Navigation" button or refresh the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
